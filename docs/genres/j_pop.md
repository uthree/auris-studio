# Genre: J-pop

Songs in verse, pre-chorus and chorus (Aメロ, Bメロ, サビ), with the chorus as the arrival: higher, wider and holding the title hook. Drop choruses and a last-chorus key change are part of the vocabulary.

## Score

- Tempo 80 to 150 BPM in 4/4; ballads 66 to 80. A full song runs intro, Aメロ, Bメロ, サビ twice, a bridge or 落ちサビ, a last サビ and an outro.
- Harmony is diatonic with a few colours: the royal road progression (IVmaj7 V7 iii7 vi), the 4-5-3-6 and 6-4-5-1 loops, a secondary dominant (III7 into vi), a borrowed iv or bVII before the chorus. Give the サビ a progression of its own; a Bメロ that moves to IV or vi and ends on V prepares it.
- The melody moves mostly in eighths, with sixteenth pickups and anticipations (食い) on key words, and long notes at the ends of lines. Aメロ sits low and speaks; the サビ lifts a minor third to a fifth on average and holds the song's highest notes.
- Look up Section roles, Chorus lift, Drop chorus (落ちサビ) and Last-chorus key change (ラスサビ転調).

## Arrangement

- Band plus keys: drums (`pop_drums`), bass (`finger_bass`), piano (`piano_pop_comp`) or electric guitar (`rock_rhythm_guitar`, `acoustic_strum`), a pad or strings (`string_legato`) in the chorus. Straight feel: swing stays at 50.
- Aメロ thin (piano and bass, hats only), Bメロ building (half time or toms, a rising line), サビ full with the ride or crash on eighths. Mark every way into a サビ with a fill and a crash.
- A countermelody on strings or a synth lead fills the gaps between the chorus lines; it never doubles the voice's rhythm.

## Mix

- The voice or lead clearly on top and in the centre, a plate-like reverb and a quarter-note delay on a return. Target -14 LUFS, -1 dBTP.
