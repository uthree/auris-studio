# Genre: Jazz

Swing eighths, ii–V–I progressions with extended chords, walking bass, comping that answers rather than accompanies, and a head–solos–head form.

## Score

- Tempo 110 to 220 BPM for medium and up swing, 60 to 90 for ballads, in 4/4. Form: the head (often AABA or a 12-bar blues), choruses of solos over the same changes, the head again. In a short piece, the head and one varied repeat.
- Harmony is sevenths and extensions throughout: ii7 V7 Imaj7, iii7 VI7 ii7 V7 turnarounds, secondary dominants (VI7, II7), iim7b5 V7 i in minor, the blues on I7 IV7 V7. Change chords every two beats in turnarounds; a dominant can take 9, 13 or 7sus4.
- The melody is phrased in swung eighths with anticipations across the bar line, chromatic approach notes into chord tones, and rests that leave the band room. Repeat a motif and vary it rather than writing new material every bar.
- Look up Colour tones, Rests and syncopation and Motif repetition and development.

## Arrangement

- Piano trio or a small combo: piano comping in rootless voicings off the beat (`jazz_piano_comp`), upright-style bass walking in quarter notes (`finger_bass` on `auris.physical.bass`), drums on the ride with hats on two and four (`jazz_drums`), the head on a lead (clarinet with `woodwind_solo`, or the piano's right hand).
- Every part swings alike on the eighth grid: swing_percent 60 to 67, swing_grid eighth, on drums, bass, piano and lead. A slower tempo takes the higher value.
- Comping is sparse and conversational: short chords between the melody's phrases, not on every beat. The bass walks chord tones and approach notes and lands on the next chord's root on its first beat.
- Look up Bass and inner voices and Voicings out of the voice.

## Mix

- Natural and open: little compression, a room reverb, the ride and the bass clear. Leave the dynamics: target -16 to -18 LUFS, -1 dBTP.
