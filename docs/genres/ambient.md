# Genre: Ambient and healing

Little or no pulse, harmony that moves slowly or not at all, long notes, and texture and space as the subject. No chorus is expected to arrive.

## Score

- Tempo 50 to 80 BPM, or a tempo only for the grid; 4/4 with phrases that ignore the bar line. Form is one slowly changing state: layers enter and leave, nothing returns as a chorus.
- Harmony changes every two or four bars or rests on one chord: Imaj7 IVmaj7, Iadd9 to viadd9, sus2 and add9 colours, a drone on the tonic under shifting chords. Avoid dominant cadences; let chords drift by step.
- Melody, if any, is fragments: a few notes from the pentatonic or the chord, long values, wide rests, repeated with small changes. Silence is part of the line.
- Look up Colour tones and Motif repetition and development.

## Arrangement

- A pad as the ground (`synth_pad` on `auris.synth.pad`, long attack and release), bell or mallet fragments (`auris.physical.bell`, `auris.physical.mallet`) placed sparsely, a piano with the pedal held (`piano_ballad`) for a few notes, a low pad note or sub-bass that changes rarely. No drums, or only a soft pulse.
- Contrast comes from register and density over the whole piece, not bar by bar: bring in one layer at a time, keep each note's start soft, and let tails overlap. Swing stays at 50; humanize the fragments so they do not sit on the grid.
- Look up Subtract before adding and Part roles and registers.

## Mix

- Wide and deep: long reverb and a slow delay on the fragments, gentle highs, no audible compression. Target -18 to -21 LUFS, -1 dBTP.
