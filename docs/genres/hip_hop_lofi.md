# Genre: Hip-hop and lo-fi

A form built on a four- or eight-bar loop that changes by adding and removing parts, boom-bap drums with a lazy swing, dusty keys or samples, and a low tempo.

## Score

- Tempo 70 to 90 BPM in 4/4.
- The harmony is one loop of two to four chords, usually sevenths and ninths (ii9 V13 Imaj9, vi9 IVmaj7, i9 iv9): keep it through the piece. Sections differ by which parts play, not by new progressions.
- The foreground is a short melodic figure on keys or a soft lead, repeated with small variations, often answered by a second figure. Leave a lot of space: a lo-fi loop is a background to stay in, not a song to follow.

## Arrangement

- Every part swings alike: swing_percent 58 to 64 on the sixteenth grid, drums, bass and keys together.
- Drums: kick on one and the and-of-two (or similar), snare or clap on two and four, hats with ghost notes and velocity variation; humanize so the hits sit loosely. Rests and dropped kicks give the groove its lazy feel.
- Keys: electric piano (`electric_piano_soul`) or a muted piano (`piano_ballad` with a short gate), voiced close and low-mid; a round bass that follows the chord roots with long notes; an occasional pad or vinyl-like texture.
- Change the loop by subtraction and addition every four or eight bars: drop the drums for a bar, bring the bass in late, filter the keys.

## Mix

- Soft and warm: roll off the highs (low-pass around 8 to 10 kHz on keys), modest reverb, drums slightly dull. Target -14 to -16 LUFS, -1 dBTP; leave the dynamics.
