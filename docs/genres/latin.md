# Genre: Latin and bossa nova

Rhythm organised by the clave or the bossa pattern, syncopated bass and guitar, seventh chords as the plain harmony, and percussion as a section of its own.

## Score

- Tempo 110 to 140 BPM for bossa nova, 160 to 220 for samba and salsa, in 4/4 felt in two. Form: AABA or verse and chorus of eight-bar phrases.
- Harmony is sevenths and ninths: Imaj7 ii7 V7, im7 ivm7 V7, iim7b5 V7 i in minor, VI7 and II7 as secondary dominants, and a chromatic line in the inner voices (Imaj7 I7 IVmaj7 ivm6). Change chords every bar or every two beats.
- The melody is smooth and syncopated, often anticipating the beat by an eighth, moving by step with occasional leaps to the ninth or the major seventh, over harmony that moves under its held notes.
- Look up Rests and syncopation, Colour tones and Entrances and anticipation (弱起・食い).

## Arrangement

- A nylon-string-style guitar (`auris.physical.guitar`) comping the bossa pattern in short chords, bass on root and fifth in the bossa's dotted rhythm (`finger_bass`), percussion from the drum kit (groove `bossa-nova`: a rim click on the clave, soft hats, a light kick), the melody on whistle, clarinet (`woodwind_solo`) or piano, strings (`string_legato`) holding a pad in the B section.
- Bossa nova keeps the grid straight (swing 50) with gentle humanize; samba pushes the sixteenths a little (swing 52 to 54 on the sixteenth grid, every part alike).
- Look up Bass and inner voices and Voicings out of the voice.

## Mix

- Warm and intimate: guitar and melody in front, percussion light and wide, a short room reverb. Target -14 to -16 LUFS, -1 dBTP.
