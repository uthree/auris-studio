# Genre: Folk and acoustic

Strummed and fingerpicked guitars, plain I–IV–V harmony, a small band, and a melody close to speech that tells a story.

## Score

- Tempo 70 to 130 BPM in 4/4, or 3/4 and 6/8 for waltzes and ballads. Form: verse and chorus, or verses only with a refrain line, and an instrumental verse between.
- Harmony is diatonic triads with a few colours: I IV V, I V vi IV, vi IV I V, a sus4 or add9 on the guitar, a borrowed bVII. Chords change on the bar; keep the bass moving between chord tones.
- The melody is narrow and singable, mostly stepwise with small leaps, phrased in two- or four-bar lines that end on long notes. A refrain repeats its tune.
- Look up Section roles, Phrase contour and climax and Cadence plan.

## Arrangement

- An acoustic guitar (`auris.physical.guitar`) strumming (`acoustic_strum`) or fingerpicking, a bass (`finger_bass`) on roots and fifths, light percussion (`pop_drums` at a low intensity, or hats alone), and one melodic colour: violin (`string_legato`), whistle (`woodwind_solo` on `auris.physical.tin_whistle`) or piano.
- Build by adding one instrument per section: guitar alone, then bass, then the second melodic voice and the percussion. Groove `eight-beat` or `six-eight`; straight feel, swing at 50, a little humanize.
- Look up Strummed chords and Subtract before adding.

## Mix

- Natural and close: the guitar and the melody in front, a small room reverb, light compression. Target -14 to -16 LUFS, -1 dBTP.
