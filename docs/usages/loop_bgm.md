# Usage: Loop background music

Music that repeats under a game or an app: the last bar runs straight into the first, nothing tires on the tenth hearing, and the foreground leaves room for what the player is doing.

## Score

- Length is a whole number of phrases, usually 16, 32 or 64 bars, and the loop boundary is a bar line. No intro and no ending: the first bar is the beginning of the loop, and the last bar leads back into it.
- End the last phrase on a chord that leads to the first (V or IV going to I, or the loop's last chord going to its first), not on a final cadence.
- A melody that is memorable without being insistent: a clear motif, then answers and rests. Avoid a single climax; the loop's high point recurs every cycle.

## Arrangement

- Keep the density moderate and steady; contrast comes from texture and register within the loop, not from building to a peak that will collapse at the wrap.
- Avoid one-off events near the boundary (a crash, a big fill) unless they belong on every cycle. The first bar must sound right after the last, including tails.

## Mix

- Leave headroom for sound effects and dialogue: target -16 to -18 LUFS, -1 dBTP, unless the user names a level. Set the loop region and check the wrap.
